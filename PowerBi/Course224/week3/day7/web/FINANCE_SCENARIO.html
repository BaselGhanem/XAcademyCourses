المالية

سيناريو اختياري لأي متدرب. لا يرتبط بمسمى وظيفي أو اسم شخص.

السؤال

أين تظهر فروقات الموازنة؟

استخدم Year Month وYear من DimDate. قارن 2026 مقابل 2025 الاصطناعية الموثقة، ولا تفترض وجود بيانات خارج الفترة.

التطبيق

ابنِ أربع KPI Cards وLine Chart شهري وBar Chart وMatrix وSlicers؛ تحقق من الأرقام قبل التنسيق.

Fact/Domain: FactFinance. Measure: [Finance Revenue].

DAX Measures

Finance Revenue

Finance Revenue =
SUM(FactFinance[Revenue])

Total Cost

Total Cost =
SUM(FactFinance[Cost])

Total Revenue Budget

Total Revenue Budget =
SUM(FactFinance[Revenue Budget])

Operating Profit

Operating Profit =
[Finance Revenue] - [Total Cost]

Operating Margin %

Operating Margin % =
DIVIDE([Operating Profit], [Finance Revenue])

Budget Variance

Budget Variance =
[Finance Revenue] - [Total Revenue Budget]

Budget Achievement %

Budget Achievement % =
DIVIDE([Finance Revenue], [Total Revenue Budget])

Finance Revenue PM

Finance Revenue PM =
IF(HASONEVALUE(DimDate[Year Month]), CALCULATE([Finance Revenue], DATEADD(DimDate[Date], -1, MONTH)))

Finance Revenue MoM %

Finance Revenue MoM % =
DIVIDE([Finance Revenue] - [Finance Revenue PM], [Finance Revenue PM])

Finance Revenue PY

Finance Revenue PY =
CALCULATE([Finance Revenue], SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR(DimDate[Date]))

Finance Revenue YoY %

Finance Revenue YoY % =
DIVIDE([Finance Revenue] - [Finance Revenue PY], [Finance Revenue PY])

Finance Revenue YTD

Finance Revenue YTD =
IF(HASONEVALUE(DimDate[Year]), CALCULATE([Finance Revenue], DATESYTD(DimDate[Date])))

Finance Revenue PY YTD

Finance Revenue PY YTD =
IF(HASONEVALUE(DimDate[Year]), CALCULATE([Finance Revenue YTD], SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR(DimDate[Date])))

Finance Revenue YTD Growth %

Finance Revenue YTD Growth % =
DIVIDE([Finance Revenue YTD] - [Finance Revenue PY YTD], [Finance Revenue PY YTD])
