السؤال

أي فئة تحسن أداؤها مقارنة بالسنة السابقة؟

استخدم Year Month وYear من DimDate. قارن 2026 مقابل 2025 الاصطناعية الموثقة، ولا تفترض وجود بيانات خارج الفترة.

التطبيق

ابنِ أربع KPI Cards وLine Chart شهري وBar Chart وMatrix وSlicers؛ تحقق من الأرقام قبل التنسيق.

Fact/Domain: FactSales. Measure: [Total Revenue].

DAX Measures

Total Revenue

Total Revenue =
SUM(FactSales[Revenue])

Transactions

Transactions =
COUNTROWS(FactSales)

Customers

Customers =
DISTINCTCOUNT(FactSales[Customer ID])

Total Quantity

Total Quantity =
SUM(FactSales[Quantity])

Average Transaction Value

Average Transaction Value =
DIVIDE([Total Revenue], [Transactions])

Revenue PM

Revenue PM =
IF(HASONEVALUE(DimDate[Year Month]), CALCULATE([Total Revenue], DATEADD(DimDate[Date], -1, MONTH)))

Revenue MoM %

Revenue MoM % =
DIVIDE([Total Revenue] - [Revenue PM], [Revenue PM])

Revenue PY

Revenue PY =
CALCULATE([Total Revenue], SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR(DimDate[Date]))

Revenue YoY %

Revenue YoY % =
DIVIDE([Total Revenue] - [Revenue PY], [Revenue PY])

Revenue YTD

Revenue YTD =
IF(HASONEVALUE(DimDate[Year]), CALCULATE([Total Revenue], DATESYTD(DimDate[Date])))

Revenue PY YTD

Revenue PY YTD =
IF(HASONEVALUE(DimDate[Year]), CALCULATE([Revenue YTD], SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR(DimDate[Date])))

Revenue YTD Growth %

Revenue YTD Growth % =
DIVIDE([Revenue YTD] - [Revenue PY YTD], [Revenue PY YTD])
