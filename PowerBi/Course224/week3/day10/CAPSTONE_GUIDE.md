# Day 10 - Capstone: From Data to Decision

**Mandatory final assessment** for all professions. Choose one question from Nova Performance, Operations, Finance or Workforce, or use another documented dataset with its own validated metrics.

## Required deliverables
1. A business question and decision owner.
2. A source/grain/key/relationship audit, including limitations.
3. Four meaningful DAX measures and one verified context or period comparison.
4. Four KPI cards, a trend and two diagnostic visuals, plus clear layout and units.
5. Two slicers and one tested Tooltip or Drillthrough with deliberate interactions.
6. A source-backed finding, recommended action and evidence-based caveat.
7. PBIX saved and reopened in Power BI Desktop, screenshots and numeric QA.

## 120-minute plan
00–10 Scope; 10–35 Data and DAX; 35–65 Report; 65–85 Interactions; 85–100 Numeric QA; 100–115 Presentation and 100-point rubric; 115–120 Submission.

### Known numeric control
January–April 2026 net transaction revenue = **12,534,018.65 JOD** under Year 2026. Day07 2025 comparisons are **synthetic**. Finance and Operations have their own fact grain; Workforce Headcount is not additive across dates.

### Optional AI visual
AI Visual Design Lab builds a **Prompt and simulated Preview**, not working DAX. AI HTML is optional, never a substitute for the report or evidence. If used, test that its values equal the Native visual under identical slicers; data should remain visible with animation off.

### Manual acceptance boundary
CI checks source packages but does **not** open Power BI Desktop, refresh PBIP, execute DAX or verify PBIX runtime. Every relevant Desktop QA test must be signed off before declaring the course production ready.
